About

A disciplined way to build.

PD Projects (Vic) Pty Ltd is a family-owned building and project management company led by Paul Paschalidis and Don Esposito, bringing together more than 25 years of combined industry experience.

Specialising in architecturally designed homes, multi-residential developments, commercial construction, and project management consultancy, we deliver high-quality projects with precision, transparency, and exceptional attention to detail.

Built on generations of trade and construction knowledge, our business combines traditional craftsmanship with modern project delivery. Our dedicated in-house carpentry team provides greater control over quality, workmanship, and scheduling, ensuring every detail is executed to the highest standard.

We believe the success of every project is built on strong relationships. By working collaboratively with our clients, architects, designers, and consultants, we provide a personalised experience from concept through to completion — delivering projects on time, within budget, and without compromising quality.

At PD Projects, our reputation is built on integrity and logical project delivery. Our goal is to exceed expectations by delivering projects that our clients are proud of and relationships that continue long after construction is complete.

Our values
